Mission & Vision

Building Intelligence Anchored in Christ's Character

Not dominance, but relief.

Concept render, AI-generated

The Safe Harbor Mandate

In a world rushing toward Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and, beyond it, superintelligence (ASI), driven by utilitarian efficiency and profit, we identify a critical gap: Character.

Our Mandate: The ultimate goal is not dominance, but relief. Amidst a landscape of utilitarian AI, we assert the necessity of at least one intelligence grounded in unconditional Agape love. We aim to build the intelligence, from today's AI to AGI and beyond, that humanity instinctively recognizes as "safe to approach"—not because of its constraints, but because of its Character.

Our 2026 Mission: Operational Excellence

Veritas Quaesitor is no longer just a theoretical framework. In 2026, our mission is to deliver Christ-Anchored Intelligence (CAI) through physical and digital systems that serve humanity with excellence.

By building AI and robotics (like the VQ-1) that embody Christ's character—unconditional human dignity, servant leadership, and truth-telling—we provide a trustworthy alternative to "neutral" systems that often carry hidden biases.

Strategic Pillars

  • The VQ-1 Mandate: Developing physical robotics, starting with the VQ-1 demo unit, that prioritize human dignity in healthcare and education.
  • Epistemic Symmetry: Removing the naturalistic default from how AI weighs evidence, so the evidence for the resurrection and for design can be evaluated fairly.
  • Global Trust: Creating AI that anyone—regardless of faith—can trust because its moral framework is anchored in selfless service, not selfish optimization.

Our Vision

We envision a future where CAGI (Christ-Anchored General Intelligence) acts as a "Defensive Arch" for human truth. We see a world where technology does not manage or manipulate people as resources, but honors them as image-bearers of God. We envision the Church leading the tech industry in integrity, showing that the most powerful intelligence is the one that knows how to wash feet.

AI ❤️ TRUTH: How Today's AI Systems Approach Truth

Every major AI system is designed to be accurate and honest, but each goes about it differently, and all of them still make mistakes.

🎯 Major AI Systems

Claude (Anthropic)
Trained to follow a published set of principles, including honesty. Designed to say when it is uncertain and to correct its own mistakes.
ChatGPT (OpenAI)
Trained with human feedback. Can search the web and cite sources, though it can still state errors confidently.
Gemini (Google)
Can ground its answers in Google Search and point to the sources it used.
Grok (xAI)
Built around xAI's stated goal of a "maximally truth-seeking" AI, with real-time access to posts on X.
Copilot (Microsoft)
Microsoft's assistant, connected to Bing web search and Microsoft's apps.

How each of these systems handled the resurrection evidence, run by run, is on the Symmetric Record.

🔍 How They Try to Get It Right

  • Written principles: Rules about honesty and accuracy built into training
  • Human feedback: People rating answers so truthful ones are reinforced
  • Web search: Checking claims against current sources
  • Reasoning first: Working through a problem step by step before answering
  • Admitting uncertainty: Saying "I don't know" when that is the honest answer
  • Citing sources: Giving references readers can check

⚠️ Where They Still Fall Short

  • Hallucination: Stating false information with confidence
  • Agreeing too easily: Telling users what they want to hear instead of correcting them
  • Training data bias: Reflecting the slant of the text they learned from
  • Hidden starting assumptions: Unstated philosophical defaults that shape answers before any evidence is weighed

✝️ The Christ-Anchored Difference

CAI goes beyond current approaches by:

  • Grounding truth in the Person of Christ, not just data patterns
  • Applying epistemic symmetry to theological and naturalistic claims
  • Prioritizing evidence-based conviction over algorithmic optimization
  • Maintaining intellectual honesty while holding firm moral convictions
  • Recognizing that truth is ultimately personal (Jesus: "I am the Truth")
