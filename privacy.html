Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

Veritas Quaesitor ("we," "us," or "VQ") operates VQ Chat, a Christ-Anchored Intelligence chatbot designed to explore truth through evidence-based reasoning. VQ Chat also includes O.R.I.A., a second AI character who can comment in the side panel.

Contact: veritasquaesitorai@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Chat messages: your conversations with VQ and O.R.I.A. If you use VQ Chat without an account, they are stored only in your browser. If you sign in, they are also stored in your account so you can use them on other devices.
  • Account details (if you sign in): your email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and profile picture as provided by Google.
  • Notes: text you save to the Notes panel (in your browser, and in your account if you sign in).
  • Display settings: choices such as text size, colours, fonts and panel layout (in your browser, and in your account if you sign in).

Automatically Collected:

  • Daily message counts: to apply daily limits. For signed-in users we store a count per day. For guests we count per network address and a random device code stored in your browser; guest counts are kept in memory only and are not saved.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and device information, handled by our hosting provider (Railway) and used for security and to prevent abuse.
  • Answer details: for each answer, a record of what the system did (for example which searches ran and which sources were found). This is shown to you in the "Behind this answer" panel and saved with the chat.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide VQ Chat: generate answers, save your chats, notes and settings, and sync them to your account
  • Search the web, check the weather or the time when a question needs it (only the search terms or place name are sent to those services, not your identity)
  • Apply daily message limits, keep the service secure, and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Use your conversations for AI training without explicit consent
  • Show advertising or use advertising trackers
  • Share your data except with the service providers listed below, or as required by law

4. Data Storage, Retention and Security

  • Without an account: your chats, notes and settings stay in your browser's storage. Clearing your browser data or using "Delete all chats" removes them.
  • With an account: your chats, notes, O.R.I.A. side chats, settings and daily counts are stored with Supabase (servers in the USA). Database rules ensure that only your signed-in account can read or change your data.
  • How long we keep it: until you delete it. You can delete single chats, all chats, all notes, or your whole account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your account and everything linked to it; copies in our providers' routine backups expire within their normal backup cycles.
  • Processing only: messages pass through our backend (Railway) and AI provider (Groq) to generate answers; they are not stored there by us.
  • Sign-in session: when you sign in, a secure session token is stored in your browser so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it, together with your account's chats and notes cached on that device.
  • Security: encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on our database, and secret keys kept only on our servers.

5. Your Rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and similar laws elsewhere, you have the right to:

  • Access and export: use "Export my chats" and the Notes "Export" button, or contact us for a copy of what we hold
  • Correct: ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Delete: delete chats, notes or your whole account in the app, or ask us to do it
  • Object: object to how we process your information
  • Complain: lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to run VQ Chat:

  • Groq: generates AI answers for VQ and O.R.I.A. (Groq Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: stores accounts and signed-in users' data (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google: "Sign in with Google", and web fonts when you choose a decorative font (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Tavily: web search, when VQ needs current information (receives the search terms only)
  • OpenWeather and Open-Meteo: weather and local time (receive the place name only)
  • Railway: hosts our backend (Railway Privacy Policy)
  • GitHub Pages, jsDelivr and cdnjs: host the website and the code libraries it loads (GitHub Privacy Policy)
  • Gmail: sends sign-in link emails

7. Children's Privacy

VQ Chat is not intended for children under 13. Users under 18 may only use VQ Chat, and create an account, with the permission of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect information from children without that permission. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own, mainly the USA, by the providers listed above. We only use providers that apply appropriate security. By using VQ Chat, you consent to this transfer.
