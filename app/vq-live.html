Working with VQ

Text stays selectable. Edit the workspace directly; “save text” downloads your notes.

Image: wheel or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. Keyboard: + / −, arrows, 0 to fit.

Chart: point or tap, or use left / right arrows. “Show values” opens the data table.

Workspace glass: the upper half of either strip scrolls up; the lower half scrolls down. Hold for 1.5× speed. Hover stops automatic scrolling for this session.

Tab moves between controls. Escape closes the current surface. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Live commands

“stop”, “retry”, “new chat”, “show response”, “next” and “workspace” control the live response. Other questions go to VQ.
